❓ Essential questionPregunta esencial

How can we use science to explain what we see in sports — and compare two examples clearly?

¿Cómo usamos la ciencia para explicar lo que vemos en los deportes y comparar dos ejemplos con claridad?

🎯 ObjectivesObjetivos

  • Content: I can explain why a spinning ball curves (spin → air speed → pressure → push) and compare the free kick with the curveball.
  • Contenido: Puedo explicar por qué una pelota que gira se curva y comparar el tiro libre con la curva de béisbol.
  • Language: I can research two sports and compare them in writing and speech with both, however, while, unlike, giving a reason with because.
  • Lenguaje: Puedo investigar dos deportes y compararlos al escribir y hablar con both, however, while, unlike, dando una razón con because.
  • Exit ticket: Two sentences: one SAME thing with both, one DIFFERENT thing with however, using spin or pressure.
  • Boleto de salida: Dos oraciones: algo IGUAL con both y algo DIFERENTE con however, usando spin o pressure.
